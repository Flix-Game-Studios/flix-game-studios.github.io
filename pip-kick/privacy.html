Pip Kick — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Pip Kick doesn’t collect anything about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and the app makes no network requests at all. Everything it remembers stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your device

All of this lives in the app’s own storage on your phone. We cannot see any of it, and deleting the app deletes it.

  • Best scores, and the score after each kick of your best run
  • Which character, hat, feather, trail and ground you’re using, and which you’ve unlocked
  • Level progress — stars earned and your best attempt at each level
  • Daily challenge results and your streak
  • Mission progress and stars
  • Whether sound is muted, and whether daily reminders are on
  • A playtest log — see below

The playtest log

The app keeps a short local record of your recent runs: how many kicks each lasted, which scoring bands the kicks landed in, and whether the run ended because you dropped it. It exists so the game’s difficulty can be tuned against how people actually play rather than against a guess.

It never leaves your device on its own. There is a “Copy playtest data” button in Records; if you press it, the log goes to your clipboard and it is entirely your choice whether to send it anywhere. The log contains no identifier of any kind — just numbers about rallies.

Daily reminders

If you turn on “Remind me about the daily”, the app asks iOS or Android for permission to post one local notification a day. The schedule is held by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push service is involved — these are local notifications only. Turning the switch off cancels it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no email, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or health data
  • No network requests of any kind — the game works entirely offline
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected

Sharing a result

If you tap Share or Copy result, the app puts an image or a block of text on your device’s share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes next is your choice, handled by whichever app you send it to. Pip Kick does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Pip Kick is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with COPPA by collecting nothing.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything — an advertising SDK would be the likely reason — this policy will be updated and the change described here before that version ships.
