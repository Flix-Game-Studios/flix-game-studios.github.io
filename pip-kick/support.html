Pip Kick — Support

Keep the shuttlecock up. Tap to kick.

Something broken, or a question? Email armanruzgar@gmail.com and say which phone you are on. There is no account to look up, so the more detail the better.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to kick. That is the whole control.
  • The lower you let the shuttlecock drop before kicking, the more the kick is worth. The glow on the ground shows the best window.
  • Swipe instead of tapping to turn a kick into a trick, worth double.
  • Chained kicks build a multiplier into a pot. Bank it to keep it, or ride it longer for more — and lose it if you drop.
  • The rally speeds up as it goes. The chevrons in the corner show which gear you are in.

Common questions

Where did my best score go? Version 1.0 made the shuttlecock fall faster the longer a rally lasts. Scores set before that change were set in an easier game, so they are kept in Records under your new best rather than being raced against.

I never see the knee or heel kick. The heel only happens on a trick, so swipe instead of tapping. The knee comes up on roughly a third of kicks by itself.

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game never makes a network request. It works in aeroplane mode.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything it remembers lives only on your phone.

Accessibility

The game honours Reduce Motion: screen shake, smear trails and the line boil are switched off when that is on in your system settings. Sound can be muted from the note button in the corner.
